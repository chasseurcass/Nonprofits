# Step-by-Step Guide to Completing IRS Form 1023

**Audience:** Nonprofit founders, executive directors, and board members. **Reference date:** October 24, 2026.

## 1. Understand the application
Form 1023 requests IRS recognition of exemption under section 501(c)(3). Full Form 1023 filing fee: $600; Form 1023-EZ (eligible organizations only): $275. Verify current fees before submission. [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023) · [Fees](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-and-1023-ez-amount-of-user-fee).

## 2. Gather documents
- Filed articles of incorporation and amendments
- EIN, legal name, address, formation date
- Adopted bylaws, if any
- Officers, directors, trustees
- Mission and detailed current/planned activities
- Actual financials and projections, as applicable
- Compensation and insider financial arrangements
- Fundraising plans and representative authorization, if applicable

Review purpose and dissolution clauses against the IRS organizational test.

## 3. Start on Pay.gov
1. Sign in at [Pay.gov](https://www.pay.gov/).
2. Find the full Form 1023.
3. Begin electronic application.

## 4. Complete each part
### Part I — Identification
Enter legal name, EIN, mailing address, tax year end, contact information and officers/directors/trustees. Reconcile with state records.

### Part II — Organizational structure
Identify entity type, formation date and jurisdiction. Prepare filed organizing documents and amendments.

### Part III — Required provisions
Locate and verify charitable purpose and dissolution provisions; identify their locations. Correct deficient organizing documents as necessary.

### Part IV — Activities
For each substantial activity describe **what, who conducts it, who benefits, when, where, how, funding, percentage of organizational time and expenses, and exempt-purpose connection**. Select NTEE code and answer applicable specialized questions. [IRS narrative guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-detail-required-in-narrative-description-of-activities).

### Part V — Compensation and financial arrangements
Disclose compensation, insider transactions, relationships, and compensation approval procedures. Check board records and contracts.

### Part VI — Financial data
Determine required actual/projected periods, prepare revenue and expense statement and balance sheet as required, and align figures with program narrative.

### Part VII — Foundation classification
Identify funding sources; evaluate public charity classification (including 509(a)(1) and 509(a)(2), where applicable); complete support information.

### Part VIII — Effective date
Check formation date and whether application is within 27 months after the end of formation month; complete Schedule E if applicable.

### Part IX — Annual filing
Determine required Form 990-series filing and exceptions. Three consecutive years of missed required filings cause automatic revocation.

### Part X — Signature
Authorized official reviews, signs electronically and certifies accuracy.

## 5. Additional schedules
A Churches; B Schools; C Hospitals/medical research; D Supporting organizations; E Late applications/reinstatement; F Low-income housing; G Successors; H Scholarships and certain grants to individuals. Check [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023) for precise applicability.

## 6. Supporting PDF
1. Combine required organizing documents, amendments, adopted bylaws, authorization forms and supplements in IRS order.
2. Keep one PDF within Pay.gov's 15 MB limit.
3. Label supplemental responses with legal name, EIN and part/line.
4. Upload. [Attachment guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-required-attachment-to-form-1023).

## 7. Leadership checklist
- [ ] Legal name, EIN, formation and address correct
- [ ] Purpose and dissolution clauses reviewed
- [ ] Governance and activity narratives complete
- [ ] Compensation and related transactions disclosed
- [ ] Financial information and classification supported
- [ ] Schedules and PDF complete
- [ ] Authorized signature and fee ready

## 8. Submit
Review, electronically sign, pay the fee, submit and retain confirmation.

## 9. After filing
Monitor IRS correspondence, respond to requests, retain determination letter and verify status in [Tax Exempt Organization Search](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/tax-exempt-organization-search). [Application status](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/wheres-my-application-for-tax-exempt-status).

## 10. Maintain compliance
File annual returns/notices, maintain board and financial records, manage conflicts and compensation, and monitor exempt activities and public inspection obligations.
